## Featured work

### PreflightOps

Pre-deployment risk assessment for SRE, DevOps and Platform Engineering teams.

PreflightOps turns a service catalog, a proposed change, Terraform signals and Kubernetes manifests into a clear operational risk score before the change ships.

It is designed for technical pre-CAB reviews, production readiness checks and pull-request risk gates.

**Highlights**

* Risk score from 0 to 100
* LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL risk levels
* Plain-English recommendations
* Rollback, monitoring, ownership and runbook checks
* Terraform and Kubernetes risk signals
* CLI, Streamlit app and GitHub Action usage
* Local-first execution with no mandatory external services

Repository: [pedroluna-gh/preflightops](https://github.com/pedroluna-gh/preflightops)
